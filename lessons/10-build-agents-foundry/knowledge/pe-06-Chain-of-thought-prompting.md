# Chain-of-thought prompting

**Category:** reasoning  
**Source:** prompt-engineering-techniques.pdf

Chain-of-thought prompting instructs the model to proceed step by step and present all the steps involved, which reduces inaccuracy and makes the response easier to assess. It is a variation on breaking the task down. Note that this technique applies to non-reasoning models; for reasoning models, attempting to extract the model's reasoning through other methods is not supported and may violate the Acceptable Use Policy.
