# Provide grounding context for reliable answers

**Category:** grounding  
**Source:** prompt-engineering-techniques.pdf

One of the most effective ways to get reliable answers is to give the model grounding data to draw from. If your use case relies on up-to-date, reliable information and isn't purely creative, provide grounding data. The closer your source material is to the final form of the answer you want, the less work the model has to do and the less opportunity there is for error. This is the core idea behind retrieval-augmented generation.
