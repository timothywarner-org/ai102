# How GPT models respond to prompts

**Category:** basics  
**Source:** prompt-engineering-techniques.pdf

Text prompts are how users interact with GPT models. Like all generative language models, GPT models attempt to produce the next series of words most likely to follow from the previous text. If you ask a question, the model isn't following a separate Q&A code path; it answers because an answer is the most likely response to that input. Keeping this fundamental behavior in mind helps as you build more complex prompts.
